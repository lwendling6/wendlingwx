What this is

You send a short message over your satellite device. A free service pulls a high resolution weather model for your exact spot, squeezes a full forecast into a few text messages, and sends it back. This app builds the request and reads the reply. It works offline once installed.

Before every trip

  1. Open the app in airplane mode. If it does not load, reconnect, open it once, and test again.
  2. Send one test request from the trailhead and decode the reply.
  3. The most recently decoded forecast is saved on this phone for offline reopening.

Requesting a forecast

  1. Pick Condensed for 2 reply messages or Full for 4 and more time steps.
  2. Pick HRRR-AK, HRRR plus Open-Meteo, or NWS grids.
  3. Tap Use my location, copy the request, and send it to wendlingwx@gmail.com.
  4. Paste every reply message into Decode. Use Paste + read when clipboard access works.

Reading this display

The top card summarizes the next 24 hours and flags major thresholds. Each day can be opened or collapsed. Rows show time, temperature, surface wind, precipitation, cloud cover, freezing or snow level, and ridge winds when those fields are present. US or metric units can be selected at the top.

If things go wrong

  1. Missing part: wait for the remaining satellite message and paste everything again.
  2. Checksum failed: re-copy the messages completely.
  3. Old forecast warning: the saved model run is aging. Request a new forecast when possible.
This is a hobby service with no guarantee. It depends on free services and models that can be wrong, especially in mountain valleys. Never base a river crossing, flight, or exposed-ridge decision on one forecast.
